How EasierLet uses AI

EasierLet uses artificial intelligence (AI) to help landlords and tenants manage properties more efficiently. Here's what that means in practice.

What AI does in EasierLet

Maintenance triage

When a tenant reports a maintenance issue, EasierLet's AI Assistant helps assess the issue, provide initial guidance, and determine whether the landlord needs to be involved. The AI makes a first assessment — the landlord always has the final say, and emergency issues are escalated immediately without AI filtering.

Property listings

Landlords can use AI to help draft listing copy — headlines, descriptions, and feature lists — based on the property details and photos they provide. All AI-generated listing content is reviewed and approved by the landlord before publication.

Portfolio reports

Landlords receive AI-generated reports summarising their portfolio: upcoming compliance deadlines, maintenance summaries, financial overviews, and relevant regulatory updates.

Tenant onboarding

When a tenant is activated, AI generates a personalised welcome email explaining how maintenance and communication works for their tenancy.

What AI does not do

  • AI does not make legally binding decisions.
  • AI does not replace a landlord's legal obligations.
  • AI content is not legal, financial, or professional advice.
  • The AI does not contact third parties, book contractors, or take any action outside the platform without landlord approval.
  • We do not use AI to make decisions about tenants' applications or personal circumstances.

Transparency and labelling

Wherever AI has contributed to content on EasierLet — in the app, in the web portal, or on public listing pages — we label it clearly with the ✨ symbol, like this: ✨ AI-assisted. You can always tell when you're reading AI-generated content.

Who provides the AI

EasierLet's AI features are powered by Anthropic's Claude AI. Anthropic is an AI safety company. Data processed by AI features is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and Anthropic's enterprise data commitments.

Your rights

If you have questions about how AI is used in connection with your data, or if you believe an AI decision has affected you unfairly, please contact us at no.reply@easierlet.com. Tenants can also request that maintenance requests be handled directly by their landlord without AI involvement at any time.
