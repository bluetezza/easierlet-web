1. Who we are and who is responsible

easierLet is property-management software run by Terry Baldwin, trading as easierLet. It's in early access, and will move to its own company before full launch; we'll update this policy when it does. "We" in this policy means Terry Baldwin, trading as easierLet. Who is responsible for your data (the "controller") depends on who you are:

  • Landlords using easierLet. We're the controller of your account, billing and support details, and of the sign-in and security records for the Platform.
  • Tenants, applicants, guarantors and referees. Your landlord is the controller of the information about you in their records: your application, references, Right to Rent check, tenancy, documents, maintenance requests and inventories. We process it for them, as their processor, under the Data Processing Schedule in our Terms. Your landlord should give you their own privacy notice. This policy explains how the software handles your data. We're the controller of your tenant portal login and of the security records for it.
  • People who request a viewing or apply through a listing. The landlord who published the listing is the controller of what you send them, and we process it for them. We're the controller of the security checks on the form.
  • Website visitors. We're the controller of the server and security logs.

For data-protection queries: privacy@easierlet.com.

2. What data is collected

If you're a landlord using easierLet

  • Your name, email, phone, contact address, company name and number
  • The properties you manage (addresses, photos, certificates)
  • Records you create about your tenants, transactions, maintenance, listings, viewings
  • Subscription and billing details (handled by Stripe — we receive metadata, not card numbers)
  • Support conversations with us
  • Audit data: which actions you took, when, and from which IP / device

If you're a tenant or applicant

  • Your name, email, phone, date of birth, nationality
  • Right-to-Rent information (visa type and expiry, if applicable)
  • Employment and income details for affordability and referencing
  • Rental history declarations (previous addresses, previous landlords, arrears, evictions, adverse credit)
  • Maintenance issues you report, replies you send, photos you upload
  • Documents shared with you for your tenancy (agreements, inventories, certificates)
  • Your tenant portal login, and audit data: which actions you took, when, and from which IP / device

If you're a referee (employer, previous landlord, guarantor)

  • Your name, email, phone, address (for guarantors), relationship to the applicant
  • Your responses to a reference form

If you visit our website without signing in

  • Standard server logs (IP, browser, referring page) for security and abuse prevention
  • If you submit a viewing request or apply for a property, the data on that form
  • Forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser for signs of automated abuse. One page (inventory signing) loads a signing component from the jsDelivr network. Both see your IP address.
  • We don't use analytics cookies or tracking pixels. See cookies.

3. Why it's used

  • Run the service. Show your data to you; let landlords and tenants do their work.
  • Tenant referencing. Verify income, employment, rental history.
  • Right-to-Rent compliance. Landlords must check and keep a copy of a tenant's right to rent under the Immigration Act 2014.
  • Legal record-keeping. Tax records (HMRC), gas safety certificates, deposit-protection records, dispute evidence.
  • Communication. Send maintenance notices, viewing confirmations, agreement signing links, password resets, billing receipts.
  • Security. Detect and prevent fraud or abuse via the audit log.
  • AI-assisted features. Some features use AI to draft content and triage requests — see section 8.
  • We do not sell personal data. We don't use it for advertising. We don't share it with marketing partners.

5. How long it's kept

The full schedule lives at Data retention periods, and the Platform applies it automatically. Summary:

  • Tenant records — 6 years after the tenancy ends, then anonymised. The landlord is told 30 days before.
  • Right to Rent details — until one year after the tenancy ends (Home Office guidance), then cleared.
  • Applications that don't go ahead (rejected, or no decision within 6 months) — 6 months, then deleted.
  • Financial records and audit logs — 7 years.
  • Gas safety certificates — 2 years; EICR — 6 years; EPC — 10 years. The current certificate for a property is always kept.
  • Viewing requests — 12 months, then anonymised.
  • Landlord accounts — while in use. If a trial or subscription ended over 12 months ago and the account hasn't been used since, it's deleted after 30 days' notice.
  • Support conversations — 2 years after closing. Data exports — 7 days.

7. Transfers outside the UK

Our main database and file storage are in London. Some of the providers above process data in the US or elsewhere outside the UK, as shown in the table. Each transfer is protected in one of two ways:

  • UK adequacy regulations. The UK government has found the destination's protection adequate. For the US this is the UK–US data bridge, which covers providers certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension.
  • Transfer contracts. The ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, built into the provider's data processing agreement.

Email privacy@easierlet.com for more detail on the safeguard for a particular provider.

8. AI features and your data

Some EasierLet features use artificial intelligence (provided by Anthropic's Claude models) to draft content and triage requests: the maintenance assistant tenants chat with, listing copy generation, landlord portfolio reports, and tenant onboarding emails. To do this, relevant data you've entered — such as tenant first names, property addresses, maintenance descriptions, and portfolio records — is sent to Anthropic for processing as our sub-processor. AI requests are not used by Anthropic to train its models under the API terms we operate on.

  • Everywhere AI-generated content appears, it's labelled with the ✨ symbol. See how EasierLet uses AI.
  • AI output is guidance, not advice, and never makes a legally binding decision about you.
  • We do not use AI to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects — landlords always make the decisions.
  • Tenants can ask for any maintenance issue to be handled directly by their landlord without AI involvement at any time.

9. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting providers. Database access rules keep each landlord's records separate, our administrators sign in with multi-factor authentication, and sign-ins and changes are recorded in an audit log. If a personal data breach affects your data, we'll tell the controller without undue delay and, where the law requires, the ICO and you.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Rectification — have inaccurate data corrected
  • Erasure — have your data deleted (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object to certain processing
  • Restrict processing while a dispute is resolved
  • Complain to the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk

Where your landlord is the controller, they decide your request, and we help them answer it. The right to erasure doesn't cover records a landlord must keep by law, or needs for the tenancy or for a possible legal claim.

11. How to make a request

Tenants and applicants: in the easierLet tenant portal, open the profile menu and use "Download My Data" for an instant copy of your data. "Delete My Account" deletes your login and anonymises your contact details straight away; if your application never became a tenancy, it's deleted in full. Otherwise your landlord keeps the tenancy records the law requires, then they're anonymised automatically. For anything else, ask your landlord. If you contact us instead, we'll pass your request to your landlord without delay and help them respond.

Landlords and everyone else: email privacy@easierlet.com with your request. We'll verify your identity and respond within one month (usually within a week). For complex requests the law lets us extend this by up to two further months, and we'll tell you if we need to.

12. Cookies

easierLet uses only the cookies and browser storage necessary to keep you signed in (Supabase auth tokens stored in your browser's localStorage) and to remember that you've seen our cookie notice. Cloudflare Turnstile may use similar storage to tell people from bots on our forms, which is needed for security. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. The notice banner on public pages is informational — nothing we set requires opt-in consent because nothing we set is non-essential.
