Data retention

How long data is kept, and why. Last updated 27 October 2026.

UK GDPR requires personal data to be kept only as long as it's genuinely needed. Some categories — like tax records — have minimum retention periods set by law. Others — like unsuccessful applications — are kept for a deliberately short window, then deleted.

For tenant, applicant and referee data, the landlord is the controller and easierLet applies this schedule for them automatically, as part of the landlord's instructions under our Terms. Everything it deletes or anonymises is recorded in our audit log.

Anonymising means clearing the personal identifiers (name, email, phone, date of birth, financial details) but keeping structural records (tenancy dates, rent, deposit) for tax and audit purposes. Once anonymised, the record can no longer be tied to you.

Tenants and applicants

DataPeriodWhy
Tenant records 6 years after tenancy ends HMRC tax-record retention + 6-year limitation period for civil claims (Limitation Act 1980). The clock starts when the landlord marks the tenancy as ended. After this, personal identifiers are anonymised; tenancy dates and amounts are kept for tax compliance.
Related tenancy records With the tenant record When a tenant record is anonymised, the tenant's documents and stored files and the signed agreement files are deleted; their signature, inventory comments, inventory photos they uploaded, maintenance descriptions and AI assistant chats are removed. The job records (dates, costs, rent) stay.
Right to Rent details 1 year after tenancy ends Home Office guidance: landlords keep copies of right to rent documents for the tenancy and one year after it. Nationality and visa details are then cleared, even though the rest of the tenant record is kept longer.
Rejected applicants 6 months Allows you to re-apply if circumstances change. After 6 months there's no ongoing legitimate interest, so the application — with its documents and references — is fully deleted.
Applications with no decision 6 months An application that hasn't become a tenancy 6 months after it was started is treated like a rejected one and deleted.
Tenant references Same as tenant record Kept alongside the tenant they relate to. Anonymised at the same time; nationality is cleared with the Right to Rent details.
Viewing requests 12 months After a year there's no operational need for prospect contact details. Names, emails, phone numbers and messages are anonymised; the booking record itself stays for analytics.
30-day notice. Before tenant data is anonymised or an application deleted, the landlord is emailed 30 days in advance. They can defer with a documented reason (e.g. unresolved dispute, ongoing claim) — otherwise it happens automatically.

Property and financial records

DataPeriodWhy
Financial records (rent, expenses, loans) 7 years HMRC requires 6 years from the end of the accounting period; 7 covers edge cases.
Gas Safety certificates (CP12) 2 years HSE: keep records for at least two years. Older certificates are deleted once they're 2 years old and a newer one is on file; the current certificate is always kept.
EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report) 6 years Five-year inspection cycle plus one year after replacement. The current report is always kept.
EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) 10 years An EPC is valid for 10 years. Older ones are deleted after that once a newer one is on file.

Accounts and the service

DataPeriodWhy
Landlord accounts 12 months without use An account that hasn't been used for 12 months (no sign-in or other activity) is deleted with everything in it. We email 30 days before; using the account in that time keeps it. Download anything you need to keep first — landlords' own legal record-keeping duties continue after they leave.
Tenant portal logins Until deleted Deleted when the tenant uses "Delete My Account", when their tenant record is anonymised, or when their landlord's account is deleted.
Terms acceptance records Life of the account Evidence of which Terms you agreed to. Deleted with the account.
Audit logs 7 years Security, regulatory compliance and dispute evidence.
Support conversations 2 years after closing Long enough to help with follow-up questions.
Data exports 7 days Download files are deleted a week after they're made.
Notifications 3 months Push and in-app notification history.
Device tokens 12 months unused Used to send push notifications; removed when a device hasn't been seen for a year.
Files left behind 1 day Stored files that no longer belong to any account are found and deleted automatically.

Earlier deletion on request

You can ask for data to be anonymised or deleted sooner. It will be, unless there's a legal reason to keep it (e.g. tax records, or a landlord's Right to Rent copies). Tenants can use "Download My Data" and "Delete My Account" in the tenant portal; "Delete My Account" deletes the login and contact details straight away. Requests about a tenancy go to the landlord, who decides them; we help. Anyone can email privacy@easierlet.com.

Backups

Our database provider (Supabase) keeps automated backups for 7 days. Anonymisation and deletion reach those backups within that window. We don't take additional offsite backups of personal data.
